Balance Schedule Privacy Policy

AUF Studio ("the developer") handles information in the Balance Schedule app ("the app") as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Events (title, date and time, category, priority, memo, place, attendees, repeat, alerts), completion records, Mind Dump notes and app settings are stored only in the app's private storage on this device.
  • This information is never sent to the developer. The developer does not run any server.
  • Notifications are scheduled on the device and are not sent anywhere.
  • The home screen widget keeps today's balance and the titles and times of upcoming events on this device only.

2. When you connect a Google account (optional)

  • If you connect a Google account for backups, the account email address is stored on the device to show it in the app.
  • Google Drive backup: only when you tap [Back up now] or turn on automatic backup, a backup file of your events, notes and settings is uploaded to the app-only folder (appDataFolder) of your Google Drive. Only this app can read and write that folder, and it does not access your other Drive files. The 5 most recent backups are kept.
  • Multi-device sync: the app reads and merges the backup files above that were made on your other devices with the same account. No other storage is used.
  • Google Calendar: designed to access only the 'Balance Schedule' calendar the app creates and its events; your other calendars are not read. This feature is not offered in version 1.0.
  • Information received from Google is used only for the features above. It is not used for advertising or any other purpose and is not shared with anyone.

3. File export and import

  • ICS and CSV export and import use only the files and share targets you choose.

4. What we do not collect

  • The app does not use advertising, analytics, location or contacts.
  • The developer cannot see the contents of your schedule.

5. Disconnecting and deleting

  • Disconnect the account: Settings → Sync & Backup → [Sign out]. You can also remove the app's access in your Google Account under 'Third-party apps & services'.
  • Erase all data: Settings → More → About → [Erase all data]. Events, notes, settings, scheduled notifications and widget data on the device are erased and you are signed out. If you choose 'Also delete backups in Google Drive', the backups in the Drive app-only folder are deleted too.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes the data on the device. Drive backups can be deleted as above or under 'Manage apps' in Google Drive settings.

6. Contact

7. Effective date

  • This policy is effective from January 20, 2027. If it changes, we will let you know in the app and in this document.
